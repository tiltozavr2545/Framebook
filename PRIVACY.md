# Framebook privacy policy

Framebook is a personal diary of film and TV ratings for Android. It has no
server and no accounts of its own. This page says what happens to your data.

## What stays on your phone

Your ratings, notes and watchlist are stored in the app's local database on
your phone. The API keys you enter (TMDB, PoiskKino, OMDb) are kept in the
phone's secure storage and are never sent anywhere except to the service they
belong to.

## Google Drive (optional)

If you sign in with Google in Settings → Sync and backup, Framebook asks for
one permission: `drive.appdata` — access to a hidden folder in your own
Google Drive that only this app can see. Framebook can't see, read or change
any other file in your Drive.

In that folder the app keeps one file with your ratings, notes and watchlist,
and up to seven daily copies of it. That's how your devices stay in sync.
The data goes from your phone straight to your Google account; the developer
never receives it and has no way to read it.

You can sign out at any time, and "Delete data from Google Drive" removes the
file and the copies. You can also revoke the app's access at
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Film data

To show titles, posters and ratings, the app sends requests to TMDB, PoiskKino
and OMDb with the keys you entered: the title you search for, or the ids of
the title you open. Those services' own privacy policies apply to those
requests. Your ratings and notes are never sent to them.

## Analytics and ads

None. Framebook has no analytics, no tracking and no ads.
