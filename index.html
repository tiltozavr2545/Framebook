Framebook

A personal diary of film and TV ratings for Android. Give your own score — to a film, a series, a single season or an episode — and see right away how it compares with Kinopoisk, IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic and TMDB.

  • Search and title cards down to single episodes, with ratings from every source.
  • Your ratings with notes, a watchlist, sorting by how far you are from Kinopoisk or IMDb, and statistics of your taste.
  • No server and no sign-up: everything lives on your phone, and syncs between your devices through a hidden folder of your own Google Drive if you want it to.
